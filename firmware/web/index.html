Each step retunes the LO and keeps the middle of one snapshot. Double-click a signal to tune there and leave the sweep. Reception was verified from 2356 to 2476 MHz; the PLL locks from about 2207 to 2795 MHz.

Analog width

Sample rate: how much you see, 80 or 16 MHz around the LO.
RC filter: what gets in. 0 lets in about 69 MHz; higher numbers narrow it. Choosing 16 Msps sets 54, so signals outside the 16 MHz view do not leak into it.
Width: picks one of two filter banks. Leave it at 40 MHz unless you are experimenting.

A gain-table index, not dB: about 1 dB a step from 35 to 76; 83 and above repeat the low end.

DC offset

Codes shift the ADC's zero by about 1.2 (A) and 0.8 (B) counts each; auto keeps the calibration.

I/Q correction

Each change reconfigures the receiver between snapshots.
